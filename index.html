Track money together, without the spreadsheet.

FinNest is an Android app for personal and household expense tracking: log expenses, split bills, track money lent and borrowed, and never miss a recurring bill.

What FinNest does

Expenses & categoriesRecord spending and see where your money goes each month.
Shared householdsAdd family or housemates and keep one shared ledger.
Splits, lending & borrowingTrack who owes what and settle up.
Recurring bill remindersAdd bills and get reminded before they are due.

FinNest does not connect to your bank accounts. Transactions are entered by you or recorded after a UPI payment you make in your own UPI app.

FinNest Premium

The core app is free. Premium is an optional, paid upgrade that unlocks:

  • Paying through FinNest via UPI, with the transaction recorded automatically after a successful payment
  • Custom expense categories
  • Households with more than 2 members
  • Push notifications for recurring bill reminders

Premium is a one-time purchase for a fixed period (monthly or yearly). It does not renew automatically. Prices are in Indian Rupees (INR) and are shown in the app before you pay. Payments for Premium are processed securely by Razorpay.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

What we collect

  • Your name, email address and profile photo (from Google Sign-In or email sign-in).
  • An optional phone number and an optional UPI ID, if you add them.
  • The financial information you enter yourself: transactions, categories, notes, balances and recurring bills.
  • Records of UPI payments you start from FinNest (amount, payee UPI ID, status and reference), so they can be recorded in your account.
  • A device token, if you enable push notifications.
  • Premium purchase records (plan, amount, date and Razorpay payment ID).

Payments

Premium purchases are handled by Razorpay. We never see or store your card, UPI PIN or bank details for Premium. UPI payments you start from FinNest are completed inside your own UPI app (such as Google Pay or PhonePe), and money moves directly from your bank to the payee. FinNest charges no fee on these payments.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.
  • We do not access your bank accounts.

Service providers

Your data is processed on our behalf by Supabase (database and login), Resend (emails you request), Google (Sign-In and push delivery) and Razorpay (Premium payments).

Shared households

Members of a household can see the shared transactions added there, tagged with the name of who added them.

Your rights and deletion

You can view and edit your data in the app at any time. You can permanently delete your account and personal data from Profile → Account settings → Delete account. Shared household entries may remain part of that household's shared record.

Children

FinNest is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.

Last updated: 1 October 2026
